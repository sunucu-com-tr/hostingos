summary, **not legal advice**; only the licence text is binding. If your
case depends on the details, ask a lawyer.

## What the AGPL means for you

- **Use is free, including commercial use.** You can run your hosting
  company on HostingOS, bill your customers through it and charge whatever
  you like for your services. There is no licence fee.
- **Running it unchanged** places no extra duty on you. Settings, content,
  themes, products and integrations that you configure in the admin panel
  are not changes to the program.
- **If you change the program** (its source code, templates, or the files
  under `static/` and `locales/`) and let people use your changed version
  over a network (your clients, your staff, visitors of the storefront,
  API users), section 13 of the AGPL requires you to offer
  all of those users the complete source code of the version you run,
  under the AGPL, for example through a link in the page footer.
  HostingOS does not add such a link on its own, so add one when you run
  a modified version.
- **If you give copies to others** (a binary, a container image or the
  source, changed or not), you must pass on the licence text, keep the
  copyright notices and make the complete corresponding source available
  under the AGPL, without adding restrictions.
- **Your data is yours.** The licence covers the program, not the
  customer data, invoices or content you manage with it.
- **No warranty.** HostingOS is provided as is (sections 15 and 16 of the
  licence).

## Commercial licence

If you cannot accept these terms, for example because you want to keep
your changes to HostingOS private while offering it to customers over the
network, or because your organisation does not use AGPL software, the
maintainer can license HostingOS to you under separate commercial terms.
Terms are agreed case by case.

## Paid services

- **Support:** help with installation, upgrades, configuration and
  incidents, with agreed response times.
- **WHMCS migration:** planning and carrying out a move from WHMCS with the
  built-in importer ([docs/IMPORT-WHMCS.md](docs/IMPORT-WHMCS.md)),
  including a trial import on a copy of your data.

## Sponsorship and donations

Sponsorship and donations are optional and never required for any use of
HostingOS. See "Support the project" in the
