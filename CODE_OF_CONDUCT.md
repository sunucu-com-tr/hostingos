# Code of conduct

HostingOS is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and
levels of experience. In issues, pull requests, discussions and every
other project space:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Criticise code and ideas, not
  people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, and no
  sexualised language or imagery.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Stay on topic and help newcomers find their way.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and
may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers (see
[COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md#contact) for the contact address, or use
the private security report form described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
if the matter is urgent). Reports are handled confidentially.
